# Exp - 4 HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

# AIM:To develop a Hospital Management System that efficiently manages patient details, appointments, medical history, doctors, nurses, prescriptions, surgeries, medicines, medical tests, equipment, billing, and payments through an integrated and user-friendly system.

# SRS (Procedure):
Patient Management: The system shall maintain patient details, complaints, medical history, appointments, and billing information.
Doctor & Nurse Management: Doctors shall examine patients, prescribe medicines, perform surgeries, and request tests. Nurses shall assist doctors, manage patients, and handle equipment.
Medicine & Test Management: Pharmacists shall provide prescribed medicines and collect payments, while lab assistants shall conduct X-ray, MRI, and other medical tests.
Billing & Payment Management: The system shall calculate and record doctor fees, medicine charges, test charges, and other hospital payments.
